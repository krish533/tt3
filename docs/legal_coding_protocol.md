# WP3 substantive legal-coding protocol (not yet executed)

Unit: an operative clause read with neighboring paragraphs and full university source policy. Do NOT label keyword hits as ownership.

For each candidate: record institution, policy year, source URL/file, precise section, quoted text, asset class; determine whether normative rule or mere definition/accounting/example; document rights-holder (university, creator, sponsor, joint, unknown); legal operation (presumption, assignment, retained rights, exception, licensing, procedure, none); triggers (employment, grant, facilities, exceptional resources); carveouts; scope of rule; legal uncertainty. A matched sentence may carry multiple rules or none.

Deduplicate policies; confirm revisions represent genuine changes rather than reprints; manually review adjacent provisions for contrary exceptions.

Validation: blinded stratified sample of flagged positives by category and decade plus random negative sentences from original corpus; double-code independently; calculate precision, recall and agreement; adjudicate disagreements, then freeze versioned codebook and classification. Report uncertain cases separately.

Historical analysis: restrict direct observed original policy vintages, never forward-filled panel years. Report both pooled and within-institution results with archives/coverage checks; do not make causal Bayh–Dole claims.

Present state: full lexical screening complete (2,779 candidate sentences in 449 of 480 policy groups). **No population-level substantive rights allocation conclusion established.**
