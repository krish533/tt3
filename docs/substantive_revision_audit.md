## Principal supported readings

* Utah changes an existing institutional title assertion and assignment-on-request into express present-assignment wording plus confirmatory assignment. This is a mechanism change, not a transition from no claim to ownership.
* The resource branch becomes narrower for ordinary departmental resources, conditional on no other ownership trigger. Compensated time remains relevant. No general contraction is inferred.
* Changing a three-year release standard to a reasonable-time standard does not introduce a previously absent release right and has no determinate direction without further facts.
* Hawaiʻi preserves a qualified creator baseline for academic and own-initiative teaching materials while specifying syllabus access/reuse and distance-learning control. More enumeration is not blanket university title.
* An earlier faculty agreement already regulates compensated recording and later sale/rebroadcast. New placement in an executive policy cannot date the first existence of those relationships.
* Hawaiʻi also expressly adds a private-time condition to one inventor exception and reduces the specified patent-related publication-delay ceiling from six months to two. These are distinct dimensions of stated control, not measured changes in actual ownership or behavior.

## Scientific disposition

This is a substantive improvement over an archive-only paper: it identifies actual differences in selected stated rules and a prior contractual arrangement that changes their interpretation. It remains a purposive comparative study with incomplete historical authentication and no independent legal validation. A reputable journal may require a larger and more systematically selected paired sample. The manuscript explicitly adopts the narrower comparative framing and is not designated submission-ready.

The linked `references/literature_gap_audit.csv` records the empirical object and limiting implication of seven close predecessors. It is a claim-boundary audit, not a declaration that an exhaustive systematic review found no equivalent study. Renewed searches targeted Halilem's title/DOI and Jamali's official article; Jamali's definition and creator-group sections were inspected directly, while Halilem's publisher page denied access. The manuscript keeps the narrower contribution rather than asserting firstness.
