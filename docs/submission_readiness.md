## Highest-priority research gap

Retrieve and authenticate comparable earlier and later sources, then obtain qualified independent contextual annotations. Reuse the frozen IDs and sampling probabilities; preserve original labels and adjudications separately. An expanded lexical screen or another LLM pass cannot resolve this gap. Access alone also does not authenticate a historical vintage. The archive can support more source research, but this release cannot honestly establish a general direction of legal change.
