## Empirical legal scholar

**Strongest challenge:** authoritative hosting does not authenticate every word as historical. Utah's 2013 PDF contains later editorial changes; Hawaiʻi has an unauthenticated intermediate vintage and separate collective agreements.

**Revision:** restrict interpretations to source-linked selected provisions, corroborate Utah against frozen sentences and its contemporaneous memorandum, preserve version qualifications, and call Hawaiʻi an endpoint comparison. Separate title, assignments, resource conditions, retained use, disclosure and publication constraints. The ledger distinguishes excerpts from the cleaned corpus from original page locators. No inference of execution or enforceability is made.

**Counterinterpretations retained:** ordinary resources may still lead to institutional title through another trigger; an expertise condition may clarify an existing employment claim; release-time work may already have counted as assigned work; a reasonable-time release may strengthen or weaken a three-year rule. The earlier Hawaiʻi faculty agreement is a precedent, not proof of identical rights for every employee through 2020. These concerns prevent a scalar net-expansion code.

**Remaining weakness:** there are no independent qualified contextual annotations. Automated checks establish reproducible links, not legal validity. The requested accuracy and agreement statistics cannot be supplied honestly.
